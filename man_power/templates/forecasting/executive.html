How these numbers are calculated
  • Each process uses its latest forecast. {{ k.mc_count }} of {{ k.process_count }} include Monte Carlo; {{ k.projection_count }} include a future projection ({{ k.aht_change_count }} with an AHT change).
  • Safe staffing = ceil(P90 FTE) with Monte Carlo, otherwise ceil(required FTE).
  • Hire now = safe staffing − current FTE for processes below it. Redeployable = whole people above safe staffing in over-staffed processes. Net hires = hire now − redeployable (not below 0).
  • Hires by {{ horizon_label }} use each process's safe staffing at that month (projected volume × projected AHT, current FTE fixed).
  • Budget = net hires × {{ money.cost_per_fte }} per FTE per year. Capacity above safe level = FTE above safe staffing × cost per FTE.
  • Health score = 40% Coverage + 30% Efficiency (100 − 2 × |utilization − {{ config.target_utilization|num:0 }}%|) + 30% Resilience. 75+ is healthy, 50–74 needs attention, below 50 is at risk.
